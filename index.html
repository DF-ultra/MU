Overview & Establishment

Mekelle University is a federal government-funded higher education institution located in Mekelle, Tigray Region, approximately 783 km north of Addis Ababa.

Formed through the merger of the Mekelle Business College and Mekelle University College, it serves as a primary academic powerhouse for the nation.

Academic Structure

The university operates across multiple specialized colleges offering comprehensive undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degrees.

  • Business & Economics
  • Dryland Agriculture
  • Health Sciences
  • Law & Governance
  • Natural Sciences
  • EiT-M Engineering
  • Veterinary Science
  • Social Sciences

Vision 2025 & Impact

Vision: To become one of the top 25 research-intensive universities in Africa by 2025.

MU actively drives local transformation through free legal aid clinics, agricultural research extensions, and Ayder Referral Hospital healthcare services.

Faculties and Campuses

Modern academic hubs engineered to foster learning, technical innovation, and clinical research.

Main Campus (Endayesus)

Serves as the university's central administrative and academic headquarters, housing foundational science, engineering, and humanities faculties.

  • College of Natural & Computational Sciences
  • College of Law & Governance
  • College of Social Sciences & Languages
  • College of Dryland Agriculture
  • Ethiopian Institute of Technology (EiT-M)

Business Campus (Adi Haki)

Specializes in business administration, economics, and commercial research, serving as a hub for entrepreneurship and management training.

  • College of Business & Economics
  • Department of Management
  • Department of Economics
  • Department of Accounting & Finance
  • Department of Marketing Management

Ayder Health Campus

Houses health sciences and medical education, integrated directly with Ayder Comprehensive Specialized Hospital—the region's primary referral center.

  • College of Health Sciences
  • School of Medicine & Public Health
  • Department of Nursing & Midwifery
  • School of Pharmacy
  • Medical Laboratory Sciences

Veterinary Campus

Dedicated to animal medicine, livestock development, and public health, delivering critical support to East Africa's agricultural economy.

  • College of Veterinary Medicine
  • Veterinary Clinical Studies
  • Epidemiology & Preventive Medicine
  • Veterinary Public Health

University Leadership & Presidents

Distinguished leaders who have shaped Mekelle University's vision, academic governance, and international stature.

Dr. Fana Hagos

Current President
2021 – Present

Previously served as President of the Tigray Regional Supreme Court for over 4 years. Holds a Ph.D. from the University of Warwick, UK.

Prof. Fetyen Abay

2020 – 2021

Tenured professor of plant breeding and seed science; recognized as the first female lecturer at Mekelle University in 1993.

Prof. Kindeya Gebrehiwot

2013 – 2020

Full professor of forestry at Mekelle University. Earned his Ph.D. from the University of Göttingen, Germany in 2003.

Dr. Joachim Herzig

2010 – 2013

Holds a Ph.D. in Microbiology and a habilitation in Human Physiology from the University of Heidelberg, Germany.

Prof. Mitiku Haile

Founding President
2000 – 2010

Widely honored as the founding president of Mekelle University. Professor of soil science and sustainable land management.
